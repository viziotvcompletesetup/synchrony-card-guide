Activate Your Synchrony Card

Fast, secure, and easy activation process

How to Activate Your Synchrony Card

Go to synchrony.com/activate using a secure browser.
Enter your card number, expiry date, and security code.
Provide identity details such as date of birth.
Click Activate and wait for confirmation.
Your card is now ready to use.

Login to Your Account

Visit synchrony.com/login.
Enter username and password.
Click login to access dashboard.
Enable security settings if needed.

Register a New Account

Go to synchrony.com/register.
Enter card details and ZIP code.
Create login credentials.
Verify your account.

Fix Common Issues

Check card details if activation fails.
Retry verification if code not received.
Clear browser cache if page not loading.
Use a stable internet connection.
